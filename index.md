# Luke Grecki

I'm a software engineer, writer, and musician based in NYC.

## Work

I've spent over a decade as a software engineer at small startups in New York City. Recently I took a year away from tech to explore contemplative care through a program at the [New York Zen Center](https://zencare.org).

## Projects & interests

- Writing [Field Notes from Silence](https://fieldnotesfromsilence.substack.com), a Substack on the inner life, published every two weeks.
- Meditation practice.
- Poetry.

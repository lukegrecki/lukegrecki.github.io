About

Hello, I’m Luke.

I’m a software engineer, a musician, and a poet. Lately my engineering work is with AI systems: agents, evaluation, and the care that tools touching people’s lives require.

I write songs on guitar, make electronic music in Ableton and on modular synths, and study tabla. Meditation and contemplative practice run underneath all of it, and shape how I build, play, and write.

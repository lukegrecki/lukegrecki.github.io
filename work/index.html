Work

Software engineering, with AI systems.

I’m a software engineer. Lately most of my work is with AI systems: agents that plan and use tools, the evaluation pipelines that tell you whether they actually work, and interfaces that help people reflect.

I care about the unglamorous parts that make these systems trustworthy: careful evaluation, observability, human review where it matters, and real attention to safety in sensitive settings like healthcare.

I’m most drawn to work where software meets wellbeing, sound, and learning.
